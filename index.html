This spring

Red fox

Cubs are being raised in an underground den. The parents are hunting overtime - if you see a fox trotting with purpose at dusk, it is probably on a food run.

Hedgehog

Waking from hibernation, thin and very hungry, and setting out on noisy nighttime rounds - a surprising amount of snuffling for such a small animal.

Common frog

Spawning season: ponds fill with croaking males and clouds of jelly-dotted spawn. A pond that was silent all winter becomes a choir almost overnight.

Hopeling School

Nothing is learned until it is seen.

That is the School's first law. So it opens with one thing to do outside, and only then with something to read. Five jobs, not a library with a lobby.

  1. Do one thing outside todayThe Errand. Today's might be: stand still for two minutes and count how many different bird voices you can hear. Names not required.
  2. ContinueThe walk you were on. Each chapter is one true idea, one thing to notice, and a field note earned that can never be lost.
  3. Five minutes of wonderOne short thing, chosen by the calendar: a bird to learn by ear from a real recording, a clue in this week's mystery, a neighbour awake right now.
  4. ExperimentThis fortnight's question in the Lab. Guess, run, open the threads, repair.
  5. Go deeperPaths and long reads, on one shelf.

The constitution

What Hopeling will never be.

  • A scoreboardNo points, no streak guilt, no leaderboards. Your tree grows rings, and a missed week is a rest.
  • An advertisementNo ads and nothing sold inside. No ranking that money could move.
  • A doom feedSeverity is shown, never performed. Every model admits what it leaves out.
  • A place a child can loseNo fail states, no timers, no chatbot. Every children's story ends in repair.
  • A collector of your lifeNo tracking. The daily companion needs no account at all.
  • Fake "live" dataThe same minute gives the same sky, everywhere. A book, not a slot machine.

These are not a mood. They are written into the app's tests, and a change that breaks one does not ship.
