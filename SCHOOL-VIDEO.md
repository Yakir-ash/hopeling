# Hopeling School - the 45-second pilot-recruitment video

Audience: ONE teacher, scrolling. Goal: not "buy this" but "lend
us your classroom for a semester." No pricing, no feature list,
no app-store badges. The ritual, not the product.

Narration total: ~95 words. Voice: the co-founder (American,
warm, a teacher talking to teachers - her "every teacher's
dream" framing, in her accent). Music: soft acoustic pulse,
no build-drop drama.

---

## Script and shots

**0-4s - the hook.**
Shot: Monday morning classroom. Kids leaning toward a projector
showing a single line: "Someone visited the garden last night.
Clue one: a feather, struck clean."
VO: "Every Monday, a mystery arrives in this classroom.

**4-10s - the ritual.**
Shot: quick cuts - Tuesday's clue, Wednesday's clue, kids
arguing gently over a suspect chart they drew.
VO: One clue a day. All week to argue about it. Friday is the
reveal.

**10-18s - the self-writing lesson.**
Shot: teacher opens the app; the sky in it matches the window
behind her. Cut to the almanac: "October: the swallows are
leaving."
VO: The lesson plan writes itself - the app runs on nature's
real clock. Real sky, real moon, whatever is alive this month.

**18-26s - the stillness.**
Shot: thirty kids holding completely still, one phone propped
up; a shy animal creeps out on screen. A held breath, then quiet
delight - no cheering.
VO: And this is the calm-down tool they'll beg for: hold still,
together, and something wild trusts you.

**26-33s - the portfolio.**
Shot: a child's field guide filling - notes, met neighbors, a
solved mystery. Parent's face at conference night.
VO: No points. No grades. A naturalist's field guide - the kind
of homework you keep.

**33-41s - the mascot.**
Shot: a real shelter dog's photo pinned above the whiteboard;
kids reading updates; final frame - adoption day, the class
cheering THAT.
VO: And the class mascot? A real shelter animal. The final exam
is her adoption day.

**41-45s - the ask.**
Shot: the Hopeling icon over warm paper, one line of text.
Text on screen: "Hopeling School - we're looking for one
classroom."
VO: We're looking for one classroom."

## Veo prompts (consistent world)

Style block to prepend to every prompt:
"Warm natural film look, soft morning light, muted greens and
cream tones, gentle handheld realism, an ordinary sunlit
American elementary classroom, diverse kids aged 8-10, one
warm teacher in her 30s, no text overlays, no logos."

1. Classroom, kids leaning forward toward a projector screen
   glowing softly, curiosity on faces, teacher smiling at the
   back. Slow push-in.
2. Three quick vignettes: a child pinning a paper feather to a
   handmade 'suspect board'; two kids debating over drawings;
   a hand crossing out a suspect. Playful, warm.
3. Teacher at the window holding a phone; through the window an
   autumn sky, on the phone the same colors mirrored. Camera
   over her shoulder, focus pull from window to screen.
4. Thirty children utterly still at their desks, one propped-up
   phone at the front, breath-held anticipation; a subtle
   collective smile spreads without noise. Static wide shot.
5. Close-up: a child's hands paging through a nature journal -
   pressed-leaf pages, careful pencil notes, a drawn robin.
   Macro warmth.
6. A printed photo of a scruffy shelter dog taped above a
   whiteboard, surrounded by kids' drawings of the same dog.
   Slow tilt down to children looking up at it.
7. A classroom erupting in joy - jumping, hugging - while the
   whiteboard reads (hand-drawn letters) 'ADOPTED!'. Handheld,
   two seconds of pure celebration.
8. Closing card: the Hopeling seed-sprout icon centered on warm
   cream paper, gentle light bloom. (Or compose this one in an
   editor rather than generating.)

## Production notes

- Cut on the narration beats; every shot earns 4-6 seconds max.
- Shot 4 (the stillness) is the emotional center - if any shot
  gets an extra second, it's that one.
- End card holds 2 full seconds of silence after the VO - the
  ask lands in the quiet.
- Deliverables: 45s master (16:9), a 30s trim for feeds (drop
  shots 2 and 5), and a 9:16 vertical crop of the 30.
- The co-founder records VO on any quiet phone memo; imperfect
  audio reads as authentic here - do not over-produce her.
